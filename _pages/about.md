I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at the [Université du Québec à Montréal (UQAM)](https://uqam.ca), and Academic Director of the CIQSS-UQAM-INRS Research Data Center. My research is in the economics of education, inequality, and labor economics, with a focus on how schooling policies shape human capital and intergenerational mobility.
