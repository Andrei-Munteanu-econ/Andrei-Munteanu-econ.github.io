I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at [UQAM](https://uqam.ca) and Academic Director of the [CIQSS-UQAM-INRS Research Data Center](https://www.ciqss.org/). I am affiliated with the [Groupe de Recherche en Capital Humain (GRCH)](https://grch.esg.uqam.ca/) and the [Research Chair in Intergenerational Economics (CREEi)](https://creei.ca/).

My research focuses on the **economics of education**, **inequality**, and **labor economics**. I study how schooling policies affect human capital formation, intergenerational mobility, and social outcomes.
